## Positioning

Branch, tag, and merge flows are part of the documented product surface, but they deserve more caution than everyday checkout and update operations during an evaluation rollout.

## Rollout advice

- Test merge paths against disposable repositories first.
- Keep history and diff views open before and after merge operations.
- Validate branch naming and destination paths carefully.
