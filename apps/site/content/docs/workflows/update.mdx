## Before updating

Review local modifications and confirm whether the update should touch the whole working copy or just specific sparse paths.

## Good habits

- Update smaller scopes when possible.
- Read conflict prompts carefully.
- Re-check history or diff views immediately if the update pulls a large set of changes.
