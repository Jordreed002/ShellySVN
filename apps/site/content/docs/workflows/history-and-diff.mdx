## What this surface is for

History and diff are the safest ways to reduce guesswork before update, merge, revert, or commit operations.

## Typical uses

- Verify what changed before an update
- Review a commit range during release prep
- Inspect file-level diffs before resolving a conflict
- Compare local changes against repository history
