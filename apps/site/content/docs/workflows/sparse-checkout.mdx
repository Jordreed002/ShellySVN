## Why sparse checkout matters

Sparse checkout is the clearest way to keep large SVN repositories usable on modern laptops. It reduces disk cost, shrinks update scope, and lets teams focus on just the part of the tree they actually own.

## Where it appears

- During initial checkout
- During later update flows
- From repository browsing when you want to add remote-only paths

## Recommended usage

Start lean. Pull the folders you need now, then expand the working copy only when a real task requires additional paths.

## Operational habit

Treat sparse expansion as part of normal repository hygiene instead of as a rescue operation after a full checkout becomes painful.
