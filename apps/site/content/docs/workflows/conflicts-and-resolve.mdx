## First rule

Do not rush straight to resolution commands. Inspect the conflict type, affected files, and recent history first.

## Practical sequence

1. Identify whether the conflict is text, tree, or path-related.
2. Open history and diff context for the affected files.
3. Decide whether to merge, revert, or re-run the operation with a smaller scope.
