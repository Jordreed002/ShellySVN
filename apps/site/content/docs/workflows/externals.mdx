## Why externals matter

Externals change what actually arrives in a working copy. Teams evaluating rollout quality should test them explicitly instead of assuming a normal checkout tells the whole story.

## Validation tips

- Inspect external definitions during evaluation.
- Confirm update behavior with externals present.
- Keep release notes clear if specific external-heavy repositories are part of rollout scope.
