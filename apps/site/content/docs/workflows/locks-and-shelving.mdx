## Important baseline

Advanced workflows such as shelving depend on the active SVN client capabilities. Treat them as environment-sensitive until your rollout validates the exact packaged client behavior you intend to support.

## Recommendation

Test locking, unlocking, and any shelving-adjacent flow against the same release artifact that your team would actually install.
