## Core expectation

ShellySVN exposes a command palette and keyboard-driven workflows so frequent SVN actions do not require hunting through menus.

## During evaluation

Validate the shortcuts that matter to your team rather than trying to memorize everything at once. Start with command access, navigation, and commit-review flows.
